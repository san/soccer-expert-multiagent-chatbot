## Fair Play and Conduct Rules

### Player Discipline

#### Yellow Card Offenses

**Unsporting Behavior:**
- Excessive celebration (taking off shirt)
- Offensive gesture
- Simulation (diving)
- Delay of game
- Entering field without permission
- Leaving field without permission
- Excessive noise/distraction

**Penalties:**
- Yellow card issued
- Accumulation: 2 yellow cards = red card
- Caution recorded in official records
- Team continues with 11 players

#### Red Card Offenses (Straight Red)

**Violent Conduct:**
- Striking opponent with fist
- Serious violent play
- Kicking opponent

**Denying Goal-Scoring Opportunity:**
- Deliberate handball to prevent goal
- Holding/pulling down striker on breakaway
- Serious foul to prevent clear scoring chance

**Spitting:**
- Spitting at opponent
- Spitting in general
- Severe misconduct

**Offense Language:**
- Racial abuse
- Offensive language
- Discriminatory conduct

**Penalties:**
- Red card issued (immediate ejection)
- Player cannot be replaced (team plays with 10)
- Suspension from next match(es)
- Possible tournament ban

### Group Stage Discipline

#### Yellow Card Accumulation

**Consequences:**
- 1 yellow card: Caution (continues playing)
- 2 yellow cards in same match: Red card (ejected)
- 2 yellow cards in different group matches: Automatic suspension for next match

**Group Stage Suspension Rule:**
- Accumulate 2 yellow cards across group stage matches
- Automatic one-match suspension
- Player misses next group match
- Resets after group stage

#### Knockout Stage Discipline

**Yellow Card Carryover:**
- Yellow cards DO NOT carry into knockout stage
- Slate wiped clean at knockout phase
- Can accumulate new cards (2 = red card ejection)

**Red Card Consequences:**
- Ejection from that match (cannot continue)
- Automatic suspension for next match (minimum)
- Possible tournament ban

### Fair Play Award

#### Purpose

The FIFA Fair Play Award recognizes:
- Teams with best disciplinary record
- Sportsmanship and conduct
- Positive player behavior
- Respect for opponents and officials

#### Criteria

**Scoring System:**
- Yellow cards: -1 point each
- Red cards: -5 points each
- Player ejections for discipline: Negative points

**Additional Considerations:**
- Behavior during/after matches
- Respect for opponents
- Conduct in victory/defeat
- Team organization and discipline

#### Winners

- Typically awarded to defensive, well-disciplined teams
- Sometimes controversial (teams that play physically but fairly)
- Recognition for tournament sportsmanship

### National Team Discipline

#### Confederation Fines and Penalties

**Based on:**
- Crowd misconduct (fan behavior)
- Official misconduct
- Player strikes or boycotts
- Team violations

**Consequences:**
- Monetary fines (€10,000-€100,000+)
- Match suspensions
- Stadium closures
- Possible tournament disqualification (extreme cases)

## Qualification Rules

### Confederation Qualification Process

#### Preliminary Round

**Teams Playing:**
- Lowest-ranked teams by FIFA rating
- Geographically-based groups

**Format:**
- Home and away matches (two-leg ties)
- Winners advance to main qualifying
- Losers eliminated

#### Main Qualification Stage

**Groups:**
- Divided by confederation
- Round-robin format (each team plays every other once or twice)
- Top teams qualify

**Advancement:**
- Top 1-2 teams per group qualify
- Usually top 2 advance
- Some confederations have additional rounds

#### Inter-confederation Playoffs

**Purpose:**
- Determine final spots for some regions
- Lower-ranked confederations compete

**Format:**
- One confederation's 4th place team vs. another confederation
- Two-leg or single-leg match

**Example:**
- Oceania winner vs. South American 5th place
- Determines final qualification spot

### Qualification Statistics

#### Participation

**Total Teams:**
- 209+ FIFA member nations participate in qualification
- Only 32 compete in final tournament
- Approximately 15% qualification rate

**Confederation Spots (Current, 32-team format):**
- UEFA (Europe): 13 teams
- CONMEBOL (South America): 4-5 teams
- AFC (Asia): 4-5 teams
- CAF (Africa): 5 teams
- CONCACAF (North/Central America): 3-4 teams
- OFC (Oceania): 0-1 teams

#### Qualification Duration

**Timeline:**
- Approximately 18-24 months
- Multiple match days (8-10 match days typical)
- Spread across calendar (international match windows)
- Ends 2-3 months before tournament

### Eligibility Requirements

#### National Team Representation

**Citizenship:**
- Player must hold passport of country represented
- FIFA registration required
- Cannot change countries (irreversible)

**Exceptions:**
- Dual citizenship: Player chooses one
- Once chosen, cannot represent other country
- Binding decision

#### Previous Tournament Experience

**No Restrictions:**
- Players can change confederation (if changing citizenship)
- Rare occurrence
- Must follow citizenship transfer rules

### Replacement Rules

#### Injury Replacement

**Procedure:**
- If player injured (documented by team doctor)
- Can be replaced by replacement player
- Medical report required
- FIFA must approve

**Timing:**
- Must be approved before tournament starts (usually)
- Cannot replace during tournament (except in extreme circumstances)

#### Suspended Player Replacement

**Eligibility:**
- If player suspended, can be replaced
- Rare occurrence (typically not allowed mid-tournament)

## Technical Rules

### Ball Control and Play

#### Offside Rule

**Offside Position:**
- Forward line: Nearer to goal than both ball and opponent
- When pass made: Must be in own half or have 2 opponents nearer to goal

**Not Offside:**
- Goal kick
- Throw-in
- Corner kick
- Own goal
- Already in own half

**Modern Interpretation:**
- Part of body where goal can be scored (head, torso, leg)
- Not offside if arms used (cannot score with arm anyway)
- Tight calls reviewed by VAR

#### Handball Rule

**Deliberate Handball Offense:**
- Hand moves toward ball
- Ball hits arm away from body
- Blocks ball with arm
- Unnatural position extends arm

**Not Handball:**
- Ball deflects off body then arm
- Arm in natural position close to body
- Very close distance (unexpected)
- Arm shields body from shot

### Playing Time and Substitutions

#### Duration of Match

**Regular Time:**
- Two 45-minute halves
- 90 minutes total playing time
- Stoppage time added (1-5 minutes typical)

**Extra Time (Knockout Only):**
- If tied after 90 minutes: 30 additional minutes
- Two 15-minute halves
- Stoppage time added

**Penalty Shootout:**
- If tied after extra time (120 minutes): 10-30 minutes for shootout
- Each team takes 5 penalties (or more if tied)

#### Substitutions

**Allowances:**
- 3 substitutions (increased to 5 in some tournaments)
- Can be made at any time (ball doesn't stop)
- Replaced player cannot return
- Each player must cross halfway line

**Modern Changes (Post-2020):**
- Increased to 5 substitutes (some tournaments)
- Addressed player fatigue concerns
- Increased tactical flexibility

## Match Regulations

### Kickoff

**Procedure:**
- Coin flip determines which team kicks off
- Loser gets choice of ends (field direction)
- Kicking off team: All players in own half
- Ball must move forward
- Scored directly: Cannot (goal kick awarded)

### Restarts

#### Goal Kick
- Ball placed in goal box
- Kicked out of penalty area
- Cannot score directly (corner kick if goes over)

#### Corner Kick
- Ball placed in corner arc (1-yard radius)
- Kicked by defending team (after attacking team last touched)
- Can score directly
- Typical delivery: High ball into box

#### Throw-In
- Ball thrown by hand over head
- Both feet on ground (or behind line)
- Thrown to teammate
- Cannot score directly (goal kick if goes over)

## Win/Loss and Advancement

### Match Results

**Possible Outcomes:**
- Win (3 points)
- Draw (1 point each)
- Loss (0 points)

**Progression:**
- Group stage: Points determine advancement
- Knockout stage: Win or go home (no draws after extra time/shootout)

### Tournament Advancement

#### Group Stage Advancement Criteria (in order)
1. Points total (win=3, draw=1, loss=0)
2. Goal difference (goals scored minus goals conceded)
3. Total goals scored
4. Head-to-head record (matches between tied teams)
5. Head-to-head goal difference
6. Head-to-head goals scored
7. Fair play points (yellow cards = -1, red = -5)
8. Drawing lots (if still tied, very rare)

#### Knockout Stage Progression
- Win: Advance to next round
- Draw after 90 minutes: Extra time (30 minutes)
- Draw after extra time: Penalty shootout
- Shootout winner: Advances
- Shootout loser: Eliminated

**Transmission:**
- FIFA controls broadcasting rights
- Television and online streaming rights granted to specific entities
- Blackout rules: None (tournament televised globally)

**Conflicts:**
- Some nations have exclusive broadcast partners
- Time zones affect viewing schedules
- Multiple streams/channels available in major countries

### Media Access

**Press and Journalists:**
- FIFA credentials required
- Accreditation process
- Access to matches and press conferences
- Restrictions: Broadcast areas, team areas

## Tournament Integrity

### Anti-Corruption Measures

**FIFA Regulations:**
- Betting restrictions on players
- Match-fixing prevention protocols
- Investigation of suspicious activity
- Cooperation with betting authorities

**Penalties:**
- Lifetime bans for corruption
- Team sanctions
- Disqualification from tournament

### Anti-Doping

**Testing:**
- Random anti-doping tests
- Out-of-competition testing
- Post-match testing

**Banned Substances:**
- Stimulants
- Anabolic steroids
- Peptide hormones
- Masking agents

**Consequences:**
- Individual player suspension
- Possible criminal charges
- Reputation damage

World Cup competition rules ensure fair play, maintain integrity, and provide a level playing field for all participating teams. These regulations have evolved to address modern challenges while preserving the sport's fundamental values.

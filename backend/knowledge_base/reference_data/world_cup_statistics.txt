## World Cup Statistics Overview

Comprehensive statistics and records from all FIFA World Cup tournaments.

## Team Statistics

### Most World Cup Wins (All Tournaments)
1. Brazil - 5 titles (1958, 1962, 1970, 1994, 2002)
2. Germany - 4 titles (1954, 1974, 1990, 2014)
3. Italy - 4 titles (1934, 1938, 1966, 1982)
4. France - 2 titles (1998, 2018)
5. Uruguay - 2 titles (1930, 1950)
6. Argentina - 2 titles (1978, 1986, 2022)
7. England - 1 title (1966)
8. Spain - 1 title (2010)

### Most Appearances in Final
- Germany: 8 finals (4 wins, 4 runner-up)
- Italy: 6 finals (4 wins, 2 runner-up)
- France: 3 finals (2 wins, 1 runner-up)
- Brazil: 7 finals (5 wins, 2 runner-up)
- Argentina: 5 finals (3 wins, 2 runner-up)

### Most Tournaments Participated
- Germany: 19 tournaments
- Brazil: 21 tournaments  
- Italy: 18 tournaments
- France: 16 tournaments
- Spain: 16 tournaments
- Mexico: 17 tournaments
- South Korea: 11 tournaments
- Argentina: 18 tournaments

### Best Finish by Nation (First World Cup Only)
- Uruguay: Champion (1930)
- France: 4th place (1938)
- USA: 3rd place (1930)
- Sweden: 2nd place (1958)

### Longest Drought (Years Without World Cup Appearance)
- Netherlands: Appeared in 8 finals total but not every tournament
- Various nations: 8+ year gaps between appearances

## Tournament Expansions

### Format Changes

**1930-1978: 16 Teams**
- Small format
- Fewer matches

**1982-1994: 24 Teams**
- Introduction of third-place playoff
- 24-team format lasted 3 tournaments

**1998-2022: 32 Teams**
- 8 groups of 4
- 64 matches
- Format now widely accepted

**2026: Possible 48 Teams**
- Expansion planned
- Exact format TBD
- More matches expected

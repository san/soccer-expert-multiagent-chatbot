## Youngest and Oldest Records

### Youngest Goal Scorers

**Pelé (Brazil)**
- Age: 17 years, 239 days
- Match: Brazil vs. Wales (1958)
- Remains youngest World Cup goal scorer
- In first tournament at age 17

**Kylian Mbappé (France)**
- Age: 19 years, 42 days
- Match: France vs. Argentina (2018 Round of 16)
- Second-youngest goal scorer (modern era)

**Other Young Goal Scorers:**
- Various players have scored at young ages
- Pelé remains all-time youngest

### Oldest Goal Scorers

**Roger Milla (Cameroon)**
- Age: 42 years, 39 days (1994)
- Oldest goal scorer in World Cup history

**Faryd Mondragón (Colombia)**
- Age: 43 years, 3 days (2014)
- Previously oldest but Roger Milla's record stood
- Recent oldest when scored

**Cristiano Ronaldo (Portugal)**
- Age: 37 in 2022 World Cup
- Oldest active goal scorer in recent tournament
